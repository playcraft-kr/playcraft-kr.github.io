KNOCKOUT

3D Boxing · Knock out all 3!
  1. Use W A S D to get close, then punch with J (left) · K (right).
  2. When your opponent pulls back a fist, a sign above their head shows how to dodge.
    ● DUCK! → Space   ● SIDE! → Q / E   ● DODGE! → any
  3. Dodge right before the hit for a PERFECT. Punch right after a dodge for 2x damage.
  4. If they cover their face with their arms, break through with L (uppercut).

The controls stay in the bottom-left corner while you play.
