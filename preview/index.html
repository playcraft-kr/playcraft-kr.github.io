About PlayCraft

PlayCraft is a home for original browser games made by young creators. Tap a game and it starts right away. There’s nothing to download and no account to make.

Every game here was reviewed by an admin first. There’s no chat and no ads.

Stay safe

  • Never type your real name, school, address, or phone number anywhere on the internet, including in games.
  • If something in a game feels wrong or scary, stop playing and tell a grown-up.
  • Games are for playing here. Please don’t copy or re-upload them. See the Terms of Use.
