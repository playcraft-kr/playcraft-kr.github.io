    About PlayCraft

    PlayCraft is a home for original browser games made by young creators. There’s nothing to download. Make a free account, tap a game, and it starts right away. Your progress follows you to any computer.

    With a free account we keep your ID, nickname, age, saved games and play time, so they follow you to any computer. Other players see only your nickname. For players under 13 we also keep a parent’s email. Read the Privacy Policy.

    Every game here was reviewed by an admin first. There’s no chat and no ads.

    Stay safe

    • Never type your real name, school, address, or phone number anywhere on the internet, including in games.
    • If something in a game feels wrong or scary, stop playing and tell a grown-up.
    • Games are for playing here. Please don’t copy or re-upload them. See the Terms of Use.
