Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

PlayCraft is a game site for young players. We collect as little as we can. If you are under 14, please read this with a parent or guardian.

1. You need an account to play

You can look around PlayCraft without signing in, but to play a game you need a free account. That way your progress is saved safely and the admin can keep the site safe. If you just look around without signing in, we don’t keep anything about you.

2. What we keep if you make an account

  • Your ID and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed); nobody at PlayCraft can read it.
  • Your nickname — please don’t use your real name.
  • Your age, so we know whether a parent or guardian needs to agree.
  • Your play records: which games you played, when, and for how long, and the games you saved with ♥.
  • Game progress and scores for games that save to your account, and practice decks you choose to share.

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, school, address or photo. There is no chat and there are no ads.

3. Why we keep it

Only to let you sign in, show your own play records on any computer, and help the PlayCraft admin keep the site safe and see which games people enjoy. We never sell it or share it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own account and records.
  • The PlayCraft admin can see the list of accounts (ID, nickname, age) and play totals, so they can look after the site.
  • Other players can’t see your account. Some games have a leaderboard or online matches with friends: there, other players see your ID and your game score, nothing else.

5. Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and play records on servers in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver fonts and code libraries. Like any website, these services see your internet address when your browser loads them.

6. Players under 14

If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree before you make an account. Parents and guardians can ask us at any time to see or delete their child’s account (see section 8).

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Play records older than 12 months may be removed. Accounts that haven’t been used for 2 years may be deleted.

8. Deleting your account

Sign in, open My Games, and press Delete my account. Your ID, nickname, age, saved games and play records are removed straight away. A parent or guardian who can’t sign in can ask for deletion by opening an issue at github.com/playcraft-kr/playcraft-kr.github.io/issues (write only the ID, nothing else personal).

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top.
