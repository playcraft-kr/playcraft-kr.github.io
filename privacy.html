Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Zanycade is a game site for young players. We collect as little as we can. If you are under 13, please read this with a parent or guardian.

1. You need an account to play

You can look around Zanycade without signing in, but to play a game you need a free account. That way your progress is saved safely and the admin can keep the site safe. If you just look around without signing in, we don’t keep anything about you.

2. What we keep if you make an account

  • Your ID and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed); nobody at Zanycade can read it.
  • Your nickname — please don’t use your real name.
  • Your age, so we know whether a parent or guardian needs to agree.
  • Your play records: which games you played, when, and for how long, and the games you saved with ♥.
  • Game progress and scores for games that save to your account, and practice decks you choose to share.
  • A parent’s email (players under 13, or older players who ask a parent to set the parent PIN): we use it to ask the parent for approval, to confirm it, to send account notices, and to send the parent a link when the child forgets their password. If the parent doesn’t approve within 14 days, we delete it.
  • A recovery email (optional, only for players 13 and over): used only to get back into the account if the password is forgotten, and for notices about the account’s security. We use it only after a code sent to it confirms it.
  • Learning mode (only if a parent turns it on): whether it is on, and the parent PIN stored scrambled (hashed) so nobody can read it. Coins spent on other games are kept with your Word League progress. We also keep the game minutes earned in Math Space War, how many paid minutes were played today, and the daily limit and school grade the parent chose.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, school, address or photo, and we never ask a player under 13 for their own email. There is no chat and there are no ads.

Ideas under games: players can write short ideas (up to 300 characters) under a game and give hearts. Ideas are shown with the player’s ID. Emails, phone numbers and links are blocked, ideas from players under 13 are checked by an admin before anyone else sees them, and players under 13 can write only after a parent approves the account. You can delete your own ideas at any time.

3. Why we keep it

Only to let you sign in, show your own play records on any computer, and help the Zanycade admin keep the site safe and see which games people enjoy. We never sell it or share it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own account and records.
  • The Zanycade admin can see the list of accounts (ID, nickname, age) and play totals, so they can look after the site.
  • Other players can’t see your account. Some games have a leaderboard or online matches with friends: there, other players see your ID and your game score, nothing else.

5. Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and play records on servers in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver fonts and code libraries. Like any website, these services see your internet address when your browser loads them.

6. Players under 13

If you are under 13, we ask for a parent’s email when you make an account. You can play straight away, but anything other players can see (your ID on leaderboards, online play, sending in a game) stays off until your parent opens the link we email them and approves. On that page the parent can also turn on learning mode with their own PIN. Parents and guardians can ask us at any time to see or delete their child’s account (see section 8).

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Play records older than 12 months may be removed. Accounts that haven’t been used for 2 years may be deleted. For each kind of information:

  • Parent email, approved: kept while the child’s account exists, to send notices and password links; deleted with the account.
  • Parent email, not approved: deleted 14 days after it was given.
  • Recovery email: kept until you remove it or delete your account. An address that was never confirmed is cleared after 24 hours.
  • Parent PIN reset links: stored only scrambled (hashed); a link works once, for 24 hours.
  • Password reset links and codes: stored only scrambled (hashed); a link works for 1 hour (24 hours when sent to a parent) and only once.

8. Deleting your account

Sign in, open My Games, and press Delete my account. Your ID, nickname, age, saved games and play records are removed straight away. A parent or guardian who can’t sign in can ask for deletion by opening an issue at github.com/playcraft-kr/playcraft-kr.github.io/issues (write only the ID, nothing else personal).

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top.
