Learning mode

How game time works · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Learning mode is something a parent turns on. With it on, you earn your game time by learning first.

In one line: the learning games are free. Every other game costs game time, one minute at a time. You earn game time in Math Space War and Word League. There is a limit for each day.

1. Which games are free?

Word League and Math Space War are the learning games. Play them as much as you like.

2. How do other games use game time?

  • When you press Play, one minute is paid. After that, one more minute is paid every minute. A minute is only paid while the game is on your screen.
  • The clock in the top-right corner of the game shows how much time you have left. It turns red for the last minute.
  • Game time is paid in this order:
    1. Game minutes from Math Space War come first.
    2. When those run out, 10 Word League coins pay for 1 minute.
  • When you run out, the game stops and shows you where to earn more.

Every new player starts Word League with 100 welcome coins, which is about 10 minutes of other games on the first day.

4. The daily limit

  • Other games can be played for up to 1 hour a day (or the limit your parent chose), even if you have more game time saved. Your saved time waits for tomorrow.
  • The day starts again at midnight where you are.
  • The learning games don’t count toward the limit.

5. For parents

  • Turning it on or off: only your 4-number PIN (or the PlayCraft admin) can turn learning mode off.
  • Changing the daily limit and the math grade:
    • For children under 13, use the parent page in the e-mail. Your child can send you a new link from PlayCraft: My Games, then “Ask my parent”.
    • For 13 and over, use “Daily limit (parent PIN)” on the account page.
    • You can choose 30 minutes to 3 hours, or no limit.
    • The math grade starts from the age your child typed (age − 5). If you set it, lowering the grade inside the game doesn’t help.
  • What we keep: the game minutes saved, how many paid minutes were played today, and the limit and grade you chose. See the Privacy Policy.
