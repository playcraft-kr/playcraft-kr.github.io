Learning mode

How game time works · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Learning mode is something a parent turns on. With it on, you earn your game time by learning first.

In one line: the learning games are free. Every other game costs game time, one minute at a time. You earn game time in Math Space War, Study Arcade and Word League. There is a limit for each day.

1. Which games are free?

Word League, Math Space War and Study Arcade are the learning games. Play them as much as you like.

2. How do other games use game time?

  • Time is counted by the second, only while a game is on your screen. If you leave the game, switch to another tab or app, or stop playing, the clock pauses.
  • Opening another game doesn’t cost anything extra: the seconds you didn’t use carry over.
  • The clock in the top-right corner of the game shows how much time you have left. It turns red for the last minute.
  • Game time is paid in this order:
    1. Game minutes from Math Space War and Study Arcade come first.
    2. When those run out, 10 Word League coins pay for 1 minute.
  • When you run out, the game stops and shows you where to earn more.

Study Arcade works like Math Space War: on a starter deck, every 10 questions with 8 or more right = 1 minute, sharing the same daily limit. Your own decks, Flashcards and Yes or No are for practice and don’t earn game time; neither do answers you mark with “Count it as right”.

Every new player starts Word League with 100 welcome coins, which is about 10 minutes of other games on the first day.

4. The daily limit

  • Other games can be played for up to 1 hour a day (or the limit your parent chose), even if you have more game time saved. Your saved time waits for tomorrow.
  • The day starts again at midnight where you are.
  • The learning games don’t count toward the limit.

5. For parents

  • Turning it on or off: only your 4-number PIN (or the PlayCraft admin) can turn learning mode off.
  • The parent PIN is set only from the link in your email, so a child can’t make one. Your child can send you a new link: PlayCraft → My Games → Your account → “Ask my parent”. The same PIN also opens the Word League reward settings.
  • Forgot the PIN? Press “Forgot the PIN?” where the PIN is asked (the account page or Word League). We email a link to choose a new one: to the parent’s email, or, if there is none, to the player’s confirmed recovery email.
  • Changing the daily limit and the math grade:
    • For children under 13, use the parent page in the e-mail. Your child can send you a new link from PlayCraft: My Games, then “Ask my parent”.
    • For 13 and over, use “Daily limit (parent PIN)” on the account page.
    • You can choose 30 minutes to 3 hours of other games, or no limit.
    • You can also choose how much game time the learning games can give each day (Math Space War and Study Arcade together, 30 minutes to start).
    • The math grade starts from the age your child typed (age − 5). If you set it, lowering the grade inside the game doesn’t help.
  • What we keep: the game minutes saved, how many paid minutes were played today, and the limit and grade you chose. See the Privacy Policy.
